# AutoADU: Newport Beach ADU research

Research checked on **November 7, 2026**. Facts marked "not verified" come from secondary sources. City pages have no dates, so confirm anything important with the Newport Beach Planning Division: (949) 644-3204.

## The problem

California law gives ADUs (accessory dwelling units) a fast path. If an ADU meets the written, objective rules, the city **must** approve the building permit ministerially: no hearing and no discretionary "no". But each of Orange County's 34 cities has its own forms, fees, checklists and readings of state law, and the state housing department (HCD) regularly tells cities they read the law wrongly. Applicants can't tell if they qualify, what to submit, or whether the city is asking for more than the law allows.

**Our user:** the applicant, meaning a homeowner or a non-profit adding an ADU.
**Our product:** a "TurboTax for ADU permits" for Newport Beach.

## What an ADU permit is (and isn't)

- An ADU is a second, smaller home on a lot that **already has a house, or will have one**. The ADU approval is the building permit itself.
- It does **not** let you build a standalone house on an empty lot.
- Types: **converted** (inside the house or an existing garage or accessory structure), **attached** (an addition), **detached** (new building), and **JADU** (junior ADU, up to 500 sq ft inside the house).
- State law lets one single-family lot combine a converted ADU, a detached ADU and a JADU (HCD letter, finding 3).

## State law (Gov. Code §§ 66310–66342, renumbered in 2024 by SB 477)

| Rule | Value | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Detached new ADU the city must approve | Up to 800 sq ft, 4 ft side and rear setbacks | § 66323(a)(2) |
| Height the city must allow, detached | 16 ft; 18 ft near major transit, plus 2 ft to match roof pitch | § 66321(b)(4) |
| Height the city must allow, attached | 25 ft or the main house's limit, whichever is lower | § 66321(b)(4)(D) |
| Max size a city may set | Not less than 850 sq ft (studio/1 bed) or 1,000 sq ft (2+ bed) | § 66321(b) |
| JADU size | Up to 500 sq ft, inside the single-family home | § 66333 |
| Parking | At most 1 space; none near transit (½ mile) and in other cases | §§ 66314(d)(10), 66322 |
| Replacement parking when a garage is converted | Not required | § 66314(d)(11) |
| Impact fees | None for ADUs under 750 sq ft | § 66324(c) |
| Owner occupancy for ADUs | Not allowed | § 66315 (AB 976) |
| Decision deadline | 60 days after a complete application | § 66317 |

- **Code:** Municipal Code § 20.48.200, and § 21.48.200 in the Coastal Zone.
- **Latest update:** Ordinance 2025-20 (PA2025-0093), adopted August 26, 2025, effective September 9, 2025 ([city rules page](https://www.newportbeachadu.org/city-rules)).
- **Main source for the table below:** the city's [ADU Ordinance Summary Matrix 2026](https://www.newportbeachadu.org/s/ADU-Ordinance-Summary-Matrix-2026.pdf). ### Single-family lot standards

| Standard | JADU | Internal ADU (converted) | Attached ADU | Detached ADU |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What it is | Inside the house, or attached to it | Converted space in the house (incl. garage) or an existing accessory building | New addition to the house | New separate building |
| How many | 1 | 1 ADU (internal, attached or detached) plus 1 JADU | same | same |
| Max size | 500 sq ft | No limit | 850 sq ft (studio/1 bed), 1,000 sq ft (2 bed) | Same as attached |
| Min size | Building code § 1208 | same | same | same |
| Max height | Per base zone | Per base zone | Per base zone | **16 ft**, with some extra allowances in § 20.48.200(F)(3) |
| Side setback | Per base zone | Per base zone | Per base zone | 4 ft or base zone, whichever is less |
| Rear setback | Per base zone | Per base zone | Per base zone | 4 ft (not on an alley) or base zone, whichever is less |
| Front setback | Per base zone | Per base zone | Per base zone | Per base zone |
| 800 sq ft guarantee | – | Floor area, lot coverage or front setback limits can't shrink an ADU below 800 sq ft on an existing lot | same | same |
| Bathroom | Can share with the house | Required | Required | Required |
| Kitchen | Efficiency (hot plate or microwave, sink, counter) | Full kitchen with outside exhaust | same | same |
| Entrance | Exterior entrance required; may also connect inside | Exterior entrance | same | same |
| Parking | None | None | 1 per unit, with waivers | 1 per unit, with waivers |
| Garage conversion | Appears to say replacement parking is required **citywide** (column layout unclear; confirm) | Replacement parking only in the Coastal Zone | – | Replacement parking only in the Coastal Zone |
| Owner occupancy | Owner lives in either unit | Not required | Not required | Not required |
| Deed restriction | Required before permit: size, no short-term rental, no separate sale, owner occupancy. More in the Coastal Zone | Not required (N/A) | Not required | Not required |
| Short-term rental | Banned | Banned | Banned | Banned |
| Separate sale | No | No, except to a qualified buyer through a qualified non-profit (Gov. Code § 66341) | same | same |
| Separate utility connection | Not required | City may require | same | same |
| Fire sprinklers | Only if the main house has them | same | same | same |
| Converted accessory building | – | May add 150 sq ft for an entrance | – | – |

**Parking waivers:** no ADU parking if the lot is within ½ mile walk of transit, in an architecturally or historically significant district, in a permit-parking area where the ADU occupant can't get a permit, within one block of a car-share spot, or when the ADU is built with a new house outside the Coastal Zone.

**Setbacks for conversions:** converting or replacing an existing structure, garage or carport needs no additional setback, though the Coastal Zone may require more.

**Basements:** a below-grade ADU or JADU in a walkout basement can be left out of the floor-area limit if it meets the criteria in § 20.48.200(F)(5).

### Multifamily lots (not in scope yet)

- Existing multifamily: up to 8 detached ADUs (not more than the number of existing units), plus converted non-livable space for at least 1 ADU and up to 25% of the unit count.
- New multifamily: 2 detached ADUs.

### HCD findings on Newport's 2023 ordinance (letter dated Oct 1, 2024)

[Full letter](https://www.hcd.ca.gov/sites/default/files/docs/policy-and-research/ordinance-review-letters/newport-beach-adu-findings-100124.pdf). HCD found that Ordinance No. 2023-2 did not comply with state law in 13 ways:

1. Allowed only "legally established" nonconforming conditions to stay uncorrected. State law protects all nonconforming conditions that don't threaten health and safety.
2. Applied the city's development standards to the ADU types state law says must be approved regardless (§ 66323).
3. Allowed only one ADU category per lot. State law allows combining them.
4. Treated a multi-unit complex as one lot, which limits ADUs.
5. Required ADUs to meet all of the underlying zone's standards. Rules outside the ADU ordinance can't be used to deny an ADU (§ 66317(c)).
6. Didn't say JADU building and demolition permits are issued together.
7. Didn't guarantee the 25 ft height for attached ADUs.
8. Applied the size cap to detached ADUs on multifamily lots, which have no state maximum.
9. Set the JADU minimum size too high.
10. Didn't exempt JADUs from replacement parking.
11. Banned selling ADUs separately in the cases state law allows (§ 66341).
12. Required owner occupancy for ADUs (§ 66315).
13. Required a **deed restriction on ADUs**. In HCD's words: "Deed restrictions are not authorized under state law for ADUs."

### What the city fixed, and what may still conflict

Comparing the 2026 matrix with HCD's 2024 letter:

| HCD finding | 2026 matrix | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 12. Owner occupancy for ADUs | Only JADUs ("Owner shall live in either unit"); ADUs N/A | **Looks fixed** |
| 13. Deed restriction for ADUs | Only JADUs; ADUs N/A | **Looks fixed in the matrix**, but the city's [permit page](https://www.newportbeachadu.org/permit) still lists a deed restriction as a required document for every application. **Mismatch between two city pages** |
| 11. Separate sale | Allowed only through a qualified non-profit (§ 66341) | **Looks fixed** |
| 3. One ADU category per lot | Still reads as 1 ADU + 1 JADU; footnote 7 says some § 66323 ADUs have "alternative requirements" | **Unclear.** HCD says a converted ADU, a detached ADU and a JADU can be combined |
| 10. JADU replacement parking | Garage-conversion row appears to require replacement parking for JADUs citywide | **Possible remaining conflict.** Confirm the column layout |
| 7. Attached ADU height | "Per base zone" | **Unclear.** HCD says the city must allow 25 ft (or the house's limit, if lower) |

**Deed restriction, exact quotes** (used by the engine check `deed-restriction`; checked 2026-11-07):
- City [permit page](https://www.newportbeachadu.org/permit), required documents (no ADU-type limit): "A deed restriction, signed and notarized, stating:" "The ADU won't be sold as a separate property" / "The ADU won't be used as a short-term rental (such as AirBnB)" / "If building a JADU, the property owner will live on the property."
- City [2026 matrix](https://www.newportbeachadu.org/s/ADU-Ordinance-Summary-Matrix-2026.pdf), Deed Restriction row: JADU = "Prior to issuance of building permit, owner must record deed restriction (prepared by the City)"; ADU columns = "N/A" (quoted from `docs/adu-application-guide.md` X1).
- [HCD findings letter, Oct 1, 2024](https://www.hcd.ca.gov/sites/default/files/docs/policy-and-research/ordinance-review-letters/newport-beach-adu-findings-100124.pdf), finding 13 (Section 20.48.200 I., Deed Restrictions): "Government Code section 66333, subdivision (c) requires the recordation of a deed restriction for JADUs, which are limited to restricting the sale of the JADU separate from the single-family residence. Government Code section 66315 requires that no additional standards, other than those provided in Government Code Section 66314, shall be used, or imposed on accessory dwelling units. Deed restrictions are not authorized under state law for ADUs. The City may not require a deed restriction prior to and as a condition of approval of an ADU building permit application."

This table is the demo's strongest material: real, sourced examples of where an applicant can get conflicting answers.

## How to submit in Newport Beach

Local copies of every form, with what each one is: [forms/newport-beach/](forms/newport-beach/README.md). The CDP handout says Coastal Development Permit applications are uploaded to the CiViC portal, with a [Discretionary Application Owner's Affidavit](https://www.newportbeachca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/71896/638239938311670000).

1. **Check the address** with the city's [Address Search Tool](https://nbgis.newportbeachca.gov/gispub/PublicAddressSearch/default.aspx) to see the zoning and whether it's in the Coastal Zone.
2. **If it's in the Coastal Zone,** a [Coastal Development Permit](https://www.newportbeachca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/51985/637805210162500000) may be needed first, which "could possibly require a hearing."
3. **Optional:** meet with city staff before applying.
4. **Prepare the package:**
   - [Plan check package](https://www.newportbeachca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/3225/636089393638270000)
   - [Building permit package](https://www.newportbeachca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/15043/635682493202100000)
   - Grant deed
   - Housing questionnaire
   - Deed restriction (see the mismatch above)
5. **Submit in person** at the Permit Center: City Hall, 100 Civic Center Drive, 1st floor, Bay C. The city says online submission through the [CiViC portal](https://www.newportbeachca.gov/civic) "may soon" be possible. Permit status can be tracked there now.
6. **Fees:**
   - Plan check: $850–$1,700, due at submission.
   - Building permit: $850–$2,100, due at issue.
   - School fees apply over 500 sq ft; traffic and transportation-corridor fees over 750 sq ft.
   - Utility fees vary. The [OC ADU calculator](https://occog.aducalculator.org) estimates the total.
7. **Review:** 10–20 days per round, and most projects need 2–3 rounds. Permitting takes 1–6 months; the whole project usually 12–18 months.

## Permits by zone and location

Checked November 7, 2026, in the current municipal code on [ecode360](https://ecode360.com/50137802) (Title 20) and [ecode360](https://ecode360.com/50143808) (Title 21, Coastal). The app looks these facts up from the address (see "Address lookup" below).

### Zoning district

| Zone (GIS code) | ADU allowed? | Permit | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Residential: `R-A`, `R-1`, `R-1-6000`, `R-1-7200`, `R-1-10000`, `R-BI`, `R-2`, `R-2-6000`, `RM`, `RM-6000`, `RMD` | Yes | Building permit only, ministerial, decided within 60 days of a complete application | NBMC § 20.48.200(C): "Accessory dwelling units and junior accessory dwelling units shall be approved in any residential or mixed-use zoning district, subject to issuance of a building permit" … "considered and approved ministerially, without discretionary review or a hearing, within sixty (60) days" |
| Mixed use: `MU-V`, `MU-MM`, `MU-DW`, `MU-CV/15TH ST`, `MU-W1`, `MU-W2` | Yes | Same | § 20.48.200(C) |
| Planned community `PC`, specific plan `SP-7` | Yes, where the plan designates residential use | Same; setbacks and height come from the PC text | § 20.48.200(A): "in areas designated for residential use, including as part of a planned community development plan or specific plan" |
| Commercial, office, industrial, open space, public (`CC`, `CG`, `CM`, `CN`, `CV`, `OA`, `OG`, `OM`, `OR`, `OS`, `PF`, `PI`, `PR`, `IG`) | No | ADU fast path doesn't apply | § 20.48.200(A), (C) |

**The zone doesn't add a different permit.** In every zone that allows ADUs, the approval is the same: a building permit through plan check. What adds permits and documents is the lot's **location** (Coastal Zone, overlays, hazards), below.

The current code also confirms (§ 20.48.200(I), Ord. 2025-20): "Prior to the issuance of a building and/or grading permit for a **junior** accessory dwelling unit, the property owner shall record a deed restriction". **No deed restriction for regular ADUs.**

### Coastal Zone

| Where the lot is (GIS `APPEAL_AREA`) | Coastal permit | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Not in the Coastal Zone | None | — |
| Coastal Zone, `NO` (not appealable) | City Coastal Development Permit (CDP) first. The Director may waive it as **de minimis** | NBMC § 21.52.035(C)(1); § 21.52.055 |
| Coastal Zone, `YES` (appeal area) | City CDP first; the decision can be appealed to the Coastal Commission; **no de minimis waiver** | § 21.52.055(B)(1): waiver only if "not located within an appeal area and not within an area where the Coastal Commission retains permit jurisdiction" |
| `PERMIT JURISDICTION` | City **Approval in Concept**, then a CDP **from the Coastal Commission** | § 21.52.015(B)(1) and (1)(a): "All development in areas where the Coastal Commission retains coastal development permit authority shall require conceptual approval from the City prior to application to the Coastal Commission." |
| `PARCEL BISECTED - SEE MAP LAYER` | Ask a planner which side applies | GIS |

**Why coastal ADUs need a CDP:** the exemption for improvements to existing single-unit homes covers garages, pools, fences and sheds, "but not including guest houses or self-contained residential units" (§ 21.52.035(C)(1)). An ADU is a self-contained residential unit. Assumption: this also covers JADUs; confirm with a planner.

**Hearings:** the city's code requires a public hearing on non-exempt CDPs (§ 21.52.015(D)(1)). State law says local governments are **not required to hold public hearings for coastal development permit applications for ADUs** (Gov. Code § 66329, as summarized by the Coastal Commission's [SB 1077 summary](https://documents.coastal.ca.gov/assets/SB1077/Summary%20of%20State%20ADU%20Law%20and%20the%20Coastal%20Act%20for%20SB%201077%20Public%20Input.pdf); statute text not read because leginfo blocks automated requests).
