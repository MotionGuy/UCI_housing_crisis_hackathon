- What data do they already have, and in which format?
- What would they need to keep using this tool after the hackathon?

## Affordability rules of thumb

- **Front-end ratio:** housing cost (principal, interest, tax, insurance, HOA) / gross monthly income. Many lenders want 28% or less; FHA allows about 31%.
- **Back-end ratio:** all monthly debt including housing / gross monthly income. Usually 36%, up to about 43% for many programs.
- HUD treats a household as "cost-burdened" when housing costs are more than 30% of income.
